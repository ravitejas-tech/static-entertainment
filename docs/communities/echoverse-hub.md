# Echoverse Hub

> Echoverse Hub is where strangers become friends, experiences become memories, and communities come alive.

The core idea: **Pune isn't boring. You're just looking in the wrong places.**

## What it is

Echoverse Hub is a community-led social experiences and events platform based in Pune. It exists to turn strangers into meaningful connections.

It brings people together through:
- curated events
- strangers meetups
- trips
- interactive experiences
- creative activities
- collaborations

These help people discover new friendships, explore their interests and feel a sense of belonging.

## What it believes

- **People first, events second.** Unlike traditional event platforms, Echoverse Hub focuses on the people and connections behind every experience. An event is how people meet; the friendship is the point.
- **Step out of the routine.** It creates spaces where people leave their usual routines, meet like-minded people and build genuine relationships beyond their screens.
- **Belonging.** Someone who comes alone should leave feeling part of something, and want to come back.
- **A better Pune.** Through its growing community, Echoverse Hub aims to make Pune more exciting, connected and full of possibilities.

## Who it's for

People in Pune who want to meet new people and try new things: newcomers to the city, people whose weekends have gone stale, and anyone curious enough to show up somewhere new.

## How its events work

Echoverse events are ticketed:

**Book → Pay → QR ticket → Gate check-in**

The platform's event types today are dance nights, social mixers, workshops, weekend trips, nightlife and meetups. Repeat attendance matters as much as first visits, so the admin's Community page tracks who is new, who keeps coming back and who to invite again.

The full product brief is [the PRD](../Echoverse_Hub_Website_Ticketing_Platform_PRD.md). What was built against it is in [the build plan](../build-plan.md).

## What this means for the website

- **Playful and high-energy.** The site's design is loud on purpose: pop colours, stickers, tilted cards, and confetti that plays once on the home page and on a new booking. It should feel like the night out, not a ticket counter.
- **Lead with people and the experience.** Photos of past crowds, shoutouts and recaps come before logistics.
- **Booking is extremely simple.** From an event page to a QR ticket in a few taps, with no account needed up front.
- **Make it easy to come back.** My Tickets, past-event galleries and the next event are always one step away.
